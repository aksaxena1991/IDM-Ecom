# Voice and tone

SkyLearn sounds like a great children's librarian: warm, curious, and respectful of the learner's intelligence.

## Kids

- Friendly, not babyish: **"Let's try again!"** not **"Oopsie wopsie!"**
- Curious and encouraging: **"What do you notice about this number?"**
- Specific praise: **"You spelled 'because' right — that's a tricky one."** instead of generic **"Great job!"**
- Errors as learning: **"Not quite — try multiplying first"**, **"Let's look again."**

## Parents and teachers

- Clear and direct, no jargon: **"Maya spent 18 minutes on multiplication today. She got 14 of 16 correct on the quiz."**

## Empty states

- Inviting: **"Nothing here yet — pick a lesson to start!"**
